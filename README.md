# ACM/PS/11 — Sustainable Stationery Brand 🌱

## Concept

A new stationery brand focused on products made from recycled materials. The goal is to create a warm, memorable visual identity and a promotional website that clearly communicates the brand's sustainability mission.

## What to Build

The website should include:

- **Brand Hero** — Brand name, mission statement, and visual identity
- **Product Showcase** — Present the stationery products in an attractive, consistent layout
- **Sustainability Story / Sourcing Process** — Explain where materials come from and how recycled materials are transformed into products
- **Shop / Call-to-Action** — Encourage visitors to explore or purchase the products

## Bonus Challenges

For an extra level of polish, consider adding:

- 🌍 An earth-toned / eco-friendly color palette and custom iconography
- ♻️ A material-sourcing infographic showing the journey from waste to stationery
- 📊 Impact statistics such as trees saved, plastic avoided, or materials recycled

> **Tip:** If using impact statistics, make sure they are based on real or clearly labeled estimated data rather than invented claims.

## Design Direction

The visual identity should feel:

- **Warm**
- **Natural**
- **Sustainable**
- **Modern**
- **Consistent**

Use earthy colors, organic shapes, clean typography, and simple environmental imagery to reinforce the recycled-material concept.

## Judges Will Look For

The project will be evaluated on:

1. **Clarity and warmth of the brand identity**
2. **Effectiveness of the sustainability storytelling**
3. **Visual consistency across the page**
